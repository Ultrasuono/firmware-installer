Install the firmware

  1. 01

    Use Chrome or Edge on a computer and a USB data cable. Turn down the audio volume.

  2. 02

    Click “Authorize Daisyseed”. With the USB dialog open, hold down the encoder for 5 seconds and select the DFU device when it appears.

  3. 03

    Click “Install mode”, then hold the encoder for 5 seconds if needed. The site waits and starts the transfer as soon as it detects the Daisy.

First installation or bootloader not found?

If the site detects only internal memory, install the Daisy bootloader before the firmware. This initial procedure requires access to the buttons on the board.

  1. On the Daisy Seed, hold BOOT, press and release RESET, then release BOOT.
  2. Click “Authorize Daisyseed” and select the DFU device.
  3. Check the confirmation below and click “Install Daisy bootloader”. This replaces the contents of internal flash.
  4. After the transfer, click “Install” for your selected firmware, then press RESET on the board. The site waits and starts the transfer automatically.
How do I enter or leave update mode?

After installing one of these firmware versions, hold the encoder for 5 seconds to enter the bootloader with no timeout. You can authorize the device and install firmware without rushing.

To leave without installing, turn Ultrasuono off and on, or press RESET on the Daisy. “Cancel waiting” stops only the website; it does not restart the device.

The first installation of this update still uses the previous firmware’s short update window. You do not need to replace the standard Daisy bootloader.

Why can’t the computer find the Daisy?

Leave the site waiting and hold the encoder for 5 seconds again. You can stop waiting with “Cancel waiting”. Check your USB data cable. On Windows, the DFU device may need the WinUSB driver: follow the instructions in the official Daisy programmer. Safari and Firefox do not support this USB installer.

Do not disconnect the device during transfer. If writing fails, the site stops. Click “Install” again and repeat the encoder press.
